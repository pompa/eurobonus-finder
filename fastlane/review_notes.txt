EuroBonus Finder is a Safari Web Extension. It checks the site you are browsing
against a list of SAS EuroBonus partner shops and their current campaigns, and
shows a bar when the shop earns points.

The app itself is just the installer and settings screen, so the extension has
to be turned on before there is anything to see:

1. Launch the app and complete onboarding (any region; Sweden has the most
   partner shops).
2. Settings > Apps > Safari > Extensions > EuroBonus Finder > turn on.
3. Under "Permissions", set "All Websites" to Allow.
4. In Safari, visit a partner shop — e.g. https://www.adidas.se or
   https://www.apotea.se — and a bar appears at the top of the page showing the
   points that shop earns.
5. Searching for a partner shop on Google marks the matching results with an
   "EB" badge.

No account or login is needed to review the app. The button in the bar opens
SAS's own public website; no credentials are required.
