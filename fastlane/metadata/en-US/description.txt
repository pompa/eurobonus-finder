EuroBonus Finder taps you on the shoulder when the shop you are browsing is a SAS EuroBonus partner — right in Safari on your iPhone and iPad.

Shopping on a phone means finding out after checkout that the store was a EuroBonus partner all along. Those points are gone for good. EuroBonus Finder makes sure that stops happening: when a site earns points, you get a quiet nudge to log in via SAS first. One tap, then shop as usual.

WHY IT EXISTS
SAS has an official EuroBonus Finder for desktop Chrome. There is nothing for the phone in your hand — the place most of us actually shop. This is the missing piece.

WHAT YOU GET
• A nudge on partner sites. A small bar appears at the top of the page when the shop earns EuroBonus points, showing what you earn.
• One tap to the SAS login, so your session starts the way that registers the points.
• Badges in Google results. Partner shops are marked right in the search results, before you click.
• The same data as the official extension. We read the partner list SAS publishes for its own Chrome extension, refreshed nightly, so shops and point rates stay current.
• Your market, your list. Sweden, Norway, Denmark or Finland.
• Out of the way. No account, no tracking, no ads.

HOW TO SET IT UP
1. Install the app and pick your region.
2. Enable the extension in Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions, and allow it on all websites.
3. Shop as usual.

EuroBonus Finder is an independent project. It is not affiliated with SAS – Scandinavian Airlines, EuroBonus, or any of the shopping partners. It uses SAS's publicly available partner data.
